Asciiland is a tiny digital forest made from nothing but text symbols. Just a small experiment in making things on the internet for the fun of it.

Made by Imtiyaz Ahmed.

/ ASCIILAND /

How to play:

Hold and drag a tree to blow wind through it, and press to shake its leaves loose. Back and Next move between trees.

How it's built:

Each tree is an illustration turned into text symbols, built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
